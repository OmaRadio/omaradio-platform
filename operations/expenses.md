# Project Expenses

Ongoing expenses related to the OmaRadio platform project. New entries are appended to the bottom of the table.

- **Date**: YYYY-MM-DD
- **Amount USD**: optional, only when the expense was billed in USD. Format: `<amount>@<exchange rate at time of entry>` (e.g. `10.00@1.35`)
- **Amount CDN**: always filled in (converted from USD using the rate in the Amount USD$ entry, or entered directly if billed in CAD)

New entries are inserted above the `—` divider row, which is kept directly above the **Total** row. The Total is kept up to date as the sum of Amount CDN$.
